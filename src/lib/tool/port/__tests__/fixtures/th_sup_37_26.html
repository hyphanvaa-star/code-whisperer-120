  MILITARY AIR EXERCISE IN BANGKOK FIR "Air Tactical Operations Evaluation"

1  INTRODUCTION

Military air exercise named Air Tactical Operations Evaluation (ATOE) will take place within the Bangkok FIR from 23 November 2026
to 4 December 2026 and 21-25 December 2026 (except Saturday and Sunday) between 0100 - 1000 UTC. Details are as follows.

4  CLEARANCE RESTRICTIONS AND FLIGHT PLANNING PROCEDURES

To improve safety, airspace capacity, and flight efficiency during the exercise, flights operating into the affected airspace must follow
the clearance restrictions and flight planning procedures outlined in this AIP Supplement.

NOTE.

- For information not listed in this AIP Supplement, refer to the information listed in AIP ENR 1.10 FLIGHT PLANNING and/or related NOTAMs.

- For flights unable to comply with the mentioned procedure, coordinate with the appropriate authority prior to submitting the flight plan.

7  AIR TRAFFIC FLOW MANAGEMENT

In response to the capacity constraint caused by the military air exercise, the following ATFM measures will be implemented:

7.1 Ground Delay Program (GDP)

The Ground Delay Program (GDP) may be implemented during the traffic congestion period. ATFM procedure is published in AIP Thailand
ENR 1.9

7.2 Temporary Flight Level orientation scheme

Flights operating on the following routes are requested to use the following flight level orientation scheme:

7.2.1  Northbound flight from VTBD, VTBS, BKK via A464 (portion BKK CMA), Y26, or Y27
and flight departs from VTPI, VTBK, VTBL, VTBH, VTPP via W9 - ODD FL

7.2.2  Southbound flight to VTBD, VTBS, VTBK, BKK via Y7, Y28 or W9 or other airways joining W9/Y28 - EVEN FL
