2.Procedures for Use of temporary training area by ACFT other
than JSDF training ACFT
(1) Training ACFT other than JSDF ACFT are required to
coordinate with the point of contact in advance for usage of
the area.
(2) ACFT not in training should not enter the area without prior
coordination with the point of contact, except ACFT flying
under IFR or ACFT mentioned below (3).
(3) ACFT except training flying the airways around the temporary
training area should report to the point of contact on
121.5MHz or 243.0MHz in case of emergency, or on
designated frequency for such as avoiding cumulonimbus
etc., when they enter the area.
3. Remarks
The altitude, exact date and time will be notified by further
NOTAM RJJJ by 24 hours before the occupied hours.

合は、 連絡先 と 事前に調整する こ と 。
（2） 訓練以外の目的で当該空域を飛行する航空機 （計器飛行方式
によ り 飛行する航空機及び下記 （3） に掲げる航空機を除 く 。）
は、 連絡先 と の事前調整な し に当該空域を飛行 し ない こ と 。

雷雲回避等の航空機にあ っ ては、 指定 さ れた周波数で連絡先
